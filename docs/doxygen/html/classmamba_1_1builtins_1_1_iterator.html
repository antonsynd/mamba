Constructs this iterator over standard C++ forward iterators which must be have a lifetime at least as long as this iterator, which is enforced by the std::shared_ptr parameters.

Note
Internally, this constructs capturing lambdas to invoke the relevant iterator methods (dereference and sentinel detection).
